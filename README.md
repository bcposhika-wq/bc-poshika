# Poshika – Personal Portfolio Website

Welcome to my personal portfolio!

This website is a reflection of who I am, what I learn, and the things that inspire me. It introduces my educational journey, personal interests, skills, creativity, and future aspirations.

## About the Project

The Poshika Personal Portfolio Website is designed to present my background, strengths, achievements, and interests in a simple and creative way.

## Website Sections

* **Home:** A short introduction about me.
* **My Story:** My personal journey, experiences, and perspectives.
* **Education:** My academic background and qualifications.
* **What I Do:** My strengths and skills.
* **Creativity:** My interest in drawing and painting.
* **Interests:** Entrepreneurship, psychology, economics, and innovation.
* **Life Beyond the Classroom:** My hobbies, reading, travelling, podcasts, and fitness.
* **Connect:** Contact information for communication and collaboration.

## Technologies Used

* HTML5 – For creating the website structure.
* CSS3 – For styling and designing the website.
* Google Fonts – For typography.
* CSS Flexbox and Grid – For layout and alignment.
* Media Queries – For responsive design.

## Design Approach

I chose a minimal and elegant design with mauve and complementary soft shades. The typography, spacing, navigation bar, and section layouts are designed to make the website visually appealing and easy to explore.
